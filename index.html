About

Disquiet Studio makes short, hand-made games about systems — the kind you learn by playing rather than by reading. No energy meters, no daily streaks, no five hundred levels. Just a handful of ideas taken seriously.

The studio is small on purpose. Everything is built slowly, and nothing ships until it feels finished.

Disquiet Studio is the storefront name of Caeiro AB, a company registered in Sweden.
